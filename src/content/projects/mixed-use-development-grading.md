Civil design for a mixed-use development combining commercial frontage with
residential above. Mixed use complicates the servicing and the grading: the parking,
loading and pedestrian approaches all need different surface treatments and
different drainage, and they meet at grade.

The design resolved the servicing layout and the surface drainage together, and
carried the package through municipal approval.
