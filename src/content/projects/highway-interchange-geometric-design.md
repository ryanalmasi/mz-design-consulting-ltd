Geometric design and drainage engineering for an upgrade to a major transportation
corridor. The work covered horizontal and vertical alignment, intersection layout
and turning analysis, and the hydraulic design of the culverts carrying cross
drainage beneath the new alignment.

Corridor modelling in Civil 3D produced the design surfaces used for earthwork
quantities and for the cross sections issued with the drawing package.
