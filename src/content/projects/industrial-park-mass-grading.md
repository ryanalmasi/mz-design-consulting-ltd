The client needed to develop a 35-hectare industrial park on highly variable terrain
against a fixed earthwork budget. The terrain fell away sharply across the eastern
third of the site, and the initial concept grading would have required a substantial
volume of imported structural fill.

We built a detailed Civil 3D terrain model from the topographic survey and tested
finished-grade alternatives against it, adjusting pad elevations and the site's
internal grade breaks to move material within the site rather than across its
boundary. Mass haul analysis then sequenced the earthwork so that cut material was
placed directly into adjacent fill areas, reducing double handling.

The resulting design reduced earthwork import by 18% against the original concept
while still meeting the finished grade requirements for every building pad, the
drainage catchment targets for the stormwater pond, and the grade limits on the
access road.
