End-to-end civil design for a commercial development in British Columbia, covering
site grading, servicing connections, on-site stormwater management, and the
submission package for municipal approval.

Coordination with the municipality ran alongside the design rather than after it, so
that the servicing and stormwater approach was agreed before the drawings were
finalised.
