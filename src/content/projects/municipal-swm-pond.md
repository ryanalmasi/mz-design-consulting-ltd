A new commercial subdivision required a municipally compliant stormwater management
facility, and the developable area left for it was tight. The concept pond footprint
consumed land the client had allocated to lots.

We modelled the contributing catchment under pre- and post-development conditions
and tested outlet configurations against the 1:100 year design storm. Reshaping the
pond geometry and revising the outlet control allowed the same detention volume and
release rate to be achieved in a smaller plan area.

The design reduced the pond footprint by 12% against the concept and was approved on
first submission, which held the development schedule.
